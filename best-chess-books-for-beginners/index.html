Looking for the best chess books for beginners? This list features 7 beginner-friendly chess books that are perfect if you're just starting out. Whether you want to build a solid understanding of chess fundamentals or prefer easy chess books that won't overwhelm you, these picks will help you improve quickly. 7. Silman’s Complete Endgame Course

♟️

Why this book is useful

With a 4.7 out of 5 review amazon and less than 5 procent of buyers giving a rating of under 4, this book is great for learning how to play endgames. What stands out about this book is that it features all levels. It is phrased: from beginner to master, meaning that you can start at an early level and continue to grow stronger and stronger with the help of this book. Each chapter represents different elo ranges. This makes it easy to train targeted, not too easy and not too hard. What especially stands out is that it is really easy to follow. One of the reviews featured a chess coach, who uses the book to train kids. He mentions that he and his students have been able to follow the book without too much effort, and that he recomends it. Other people like the fact that it covers practical endgames, rather than endgames you will never see on the board, such as Bishop Knight checkmate. I am a 1900 rated chess player, and I played a lot of tournaments, and I can confirm that I have never seen a Bishop Knight Checkmate, or one of those endgame studies other books cover. In reality, you will see that the endgame you will get by far the most are rook endgames and king and pawn endgames, which are greatly covered in this book. You will really learn ideas that win you the game, rather than theoretical wins that only become significant at a higher level.
