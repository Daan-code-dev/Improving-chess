Important Chess Tactical Motifs Explained

Almost every game below 1600 rating comes down to these tactical motifs. Knowing them isn't just about spotting your own chances — recognizing them also helps you defend when your opponent tries the same thing. As a ~2000 rated player, I have seen these patterns more times than I can count, both as the attacker and the defender. On this page, I break down the most important motifs so you can start spotting them yourself.
