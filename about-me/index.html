About Me

Who I Am

I have been playing chess for over 10 years now. The first few years I mainly played with my father and brother, and after that online and even tournaments. My chess.com rating is ~2150, and I have won a regular and blitz national championship when I was really young, and I represented my country at the European Championship when I was younger. We once had chess lessons in school, and that's also when I decided to join a chess club. I even became club champion of the adults in my youth, which was really cool. Nowadays I don't play official tournaments, but I still regularly play online.

Why I Created Improving Chess

About a year ago, I started learning web design, from a free online course. I liked it, and I wanted to practise my skills by building one page, only one. I started thinking, what can I write about? Naturally, I came to chess. The first page that I wrote was my best chess tactics books. I included affiliate links, because I thought: Two birds with one stone. And then I decided to make a second page. And a third page. You can see how it developed. At the time of writing this, this is the tenth page of my website. So that's the brutally honest why.

However, instead of just spamming affiliate links, what my original plan was, I decided to balance it with truly helpful content. And boy, I didn't know getting a website to rank was this difficult. I know that I'm not the best at optimizing my page, and I'm definitely not the best developer out there, but I think I do have content of good quality, written by an experienced chess player. That's what I am going to build upon.

My Chess Experience

I have a variety of different chess experiences worth mentioning, but first; I am not Magnus Carlsen, I don't have a 2600 FIDE rating and I am not the best there is. However, I do have a lot of experience. When I was younger, I have played against and beaten FMs, represented my country at the European Championship, won a regular and blitz national championship, and went on to win the club championship of the adults. And who knows how many GothamChess videos I have seen. So I do have the experience and knowledge to help players improve.

In my articles, I use my experience that I have built up over the years. That's how I know that knight and bishop mate is super uncommon, and tactics decide most games. With my 2150 rating on chess.com, I am not the best chess player in the world, but I have enough experience to help players get better.

What You Can Find Here

My website is still really small compared with how I eventually want it to be, and I can't make promises about what it will look like in the future. At the time of writing this, my focus lies on beginner content and tactics content, but when you're reading this, I might have endgames, openings or strategy. My main focus will stay the same; make helpful content. Whether it's endgames or a specific opening, I am writing it for people to learn from.

My Goal With Improving Chess

My goal is to build a site where players can come when they want to get better at chess, and where I can share my expertise with other people. And, of course, I'd like to make some money from it too. I mean, who doesn't like money? :)

If you're new to chess, you can start with my beginner's guide. If you are looking to improve your tactical vision, check out the tactics section.
